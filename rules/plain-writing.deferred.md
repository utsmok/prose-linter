The writing skill merges this skill together with unslop and
technical-writing, and it supersedes plain-writing as the style reference.
The prose-linter extension injects it into every session's system prompt; on
installs without the extension, `rules/writing.md` is the pointer instead.

The `/plain-writing deslopify` command in the plain-writing skill still
works as before.
